🚀 PromoTrack

A web app I built to help my colleagues get promoted to their next job level. From blank page to complete promotion document — guided every step of the way.

React + TypeScript + AI-Powered + AWS Serverless

The Problem

Getting promoted is hard — not because people aren't ready, but because the process is overwhelming and scattered.

😰

"I don't know where to start"

The promotion process involves dozens of steps across wikis, templates, and tribal knowledge.

✍️

"I can't write good STAR entries"

Writing compelling narratives that demonstrate Leadership Principles is a skill most people haven't practiced.

📊

"Are my metrics good enough?"

Performance data is scattered across dashboards. Hard to know if you meet next-level expectations.

🔄

"Getting feedback takes forever"

Sharing drafts via email leads to version confusion and delayed manager responses.

📋

"What gaps do I have?"

Without a clear view of LP coverage, it's easy to miss critical areas in your portfolio.

📄

"The final document is painful"

Assembling everything into the correct promotion document format is tedious manual work.

How PromoTrack Helps

Every feature removes friction from the promotion journey.

📊 Dashboard — Know Where You Stand

LP coverage radar chart shows which Leadership Principles you've demonstrated. Portfolio completion checklist tracks progress. Set your target date and see a countdown to stay focused.

⭐ STAR Narratives — Guided Writing

Don't stare at a blank page. PromoTrack breaks writing into promotion dimensions (Ambiguity, Scope, Execution, Impact...) with clear prompts. Pick a category, use a template, or start fresh.

🤖 AI Writing Assistant

Write your rough draft, then let AI transform it into polished, promotion-ready language. Give custom instructions and selectively apply improvements section by section.

📈 Metrics Scorecard

Import your GSD Scorecard PDF with one click. Visual progress bars show green (meeting target) or red (below target). Know instantly where you stand against next-level expectations.

📄 One-Click Document Generation

Preview and generate your promotion document in the official format. Employee info, scope of role, performance strengths, and STAR narratives — assembled automatically.

👥 Manager Review Workflow

Share your portfolio via a single link. Managers review each STAR entry and leave feedback directly in the app. Comments appear on your entries — no email back-and-forth.
